\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have presented NepGraph, a reproducible correlation-based framework
for discovering hidden community structure in the Nepal Stock
Exchange.  The framework couples the Mantegna distance, a top-$k$
sparsification, the Minimum Spanning Tree, and the Louvain
community-detection algorithm, and exposes the resulting partition
through an interactive dashboard.  A pre-registered $k$-sweep over
$440$ parameter combinations justifies $k=5$ as the minimum
sparsification producing a single connected graph across all temporal
windows, yielding $9$--$11$ communities with modularity
$Q\in[0.62,0.66]$.  Statistical significance is confirmed by an
edge-swap null model ($z=82.5$).  The detected communities overlap
only partially with the official sector map ($\text{ARI}=0.21$), and
the mismatches can be surfaced as a transparent anomaly score.

Future work will (i)~extend the framework to the Planar Maximally
Filtered Graph (PMFG) for higher-resolution cluster detection,
(ii)~replace the static Pearson correlation with a DCC-GARCH-driven
dynamic correlation network, and (iii)~validate the anomaly score
against labelled market manipulation cases on NEPSE.  We hope that
releasing the entire pipeline under MIT lowers the bar for further
quantitative research on this frontier market.
